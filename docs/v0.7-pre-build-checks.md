# v0.7 pre-build checks

No board has been built from the 2024 design or from its KiCad port. The only Okapi boards that exist are the 25 from 2020 (v0.4), and their schematic is a different one. So nothing below has an as-built value yet: this is what to confirm on the first v0.7.0 boards, with the value the design expects beside each.

Measure two or three boards from the run, so that one odd board does not stand for all of them.

## 1. Look (unpowered)

1. R9, R36–R40, R42–R44: 0402 (1.0 × 0.5 mm), not 0603 (1.6 × 0.8 mm). An 0603 on these pads overhangs them.
2. C40, C41, C42: 0402. Record the markings on the reels, since a ceramic capacitor carries none.
3. R48: record the size. It is 0402 as built, and 0603 only if someone has swapped it for more reference current (a 330 Ω 0603, `RC0603FR-07330RL`, fits the same pads).
4. R22 (0603) and R69 (0402), marked on the silk as the MPPT pair; R70 and R71 0402. All four sit on dual lands that take either size.
5. Q7, Q4, Q8: record the top marking. The BoM change is for Q7; Q4 and Q8 carried the same part number.
6. R72–R75 and the charger sense resistor: 0805. Record any marking.
7. R5, R7, R9, R11, R15, R81–R83: 0402. C1, C22: 0603. C15, C16: 0402. C3, C4, C14, C30: 0805 22 µF (record the reel). C43: a ceramic 1206 on the tantalum land, centered.

## 2. Resistance (unpowered, batteries, USB and solar input out)

Many of these sit between unpowered ICs, whose protection diodes can conduct at an ohmmeter's test voltage. Take each reading in both probe polarities and record the higher one. Measure the four shunts with a four-wire (Kelvin) milliohm meter on the pads; a two-wire meter cannot read 20 mΩ.

## 3. Powered

1. **Port current limits (optional, with a current-limited load).** Enable one port switch and load its output with an electronic load in constant-current mode. Raise the current until the output collapses and record that trip current. The datasheet gives 0.79–1.19 A with the jumper open and 1.04–1.93 A with it closed (R_SET = 230 V / I, with the current-limit factor spread 184–276 V up to 1 A and 161–299 V above it). If the upstream rail sags first, record where.
2. **R19.** With FEATHER_EN not driven, measure the voltage across R19. The current is that voltage divided by the reading from step 2. When firmware drives FEATHER_EN low, the pull-up carries about 33 µA at 3.3 V.
3. **R48 and the reference.** With 5V_AUX on, measure VREF (J2 pin 2) and the voltage across R48. Expect 2.5 V on VREF and about 2.5 mA through R48 (the LM4040 holds 2.5 V from 60 µA to 15 mA). Then load VREF with 1.25 kΩ (about 2 mA) and record VREF again.
4. **R51 and the PAC1934.** Scan the I2C bus on the segment the PAC1934 sits on. Expect an answer at 0x10 and the product ID 0x5B from `pacFind()`; the other chips are at 0x48, 0x52, 0x20, 0x50, 0x77, 0x0C, 0x68 and 0x18 (the BMA456's other strap would put it at 0x19).
5. **Shunts and zero offset.** With the load off, read each channel's current. The PAC1934's offset is ±0.02 mV at 25 °C, which is ±1 mA on 20 mΩ and ±0.4 mA on 50 mΩ. Then pass a known current through each channel (about 1 A, from a bench supply read on a second meter) and compare: expect 20 mV across a 20 mΩ shunt and 50 mV across a 50 mΩ one. On V_LI the current is bipolar: charging reads positive.
6. **MPPT pair.** Feed the solar input from a current-limited bench supply and lower its voltage below the point the supply can sustain. The charger holds the input at VIN_REG, 2.7 V × (1 + 143 kΩ / 100 kΩ) = 6.56 V by the design. Record where it settles.
7. **Q7.** Command 5V_AUX off and measure the current from the backup battery. A latched Q7 draws about 100 mA; expect the sleep current instead.

The PAC1934 path in the firmware (`Okapi.cpp`) has not run on hardware.
